The Experience & Setup

We bring the bar and experienced bartenders straight to you! Here is what is included:

  • Ice, house-made mixers, garnishes, and professional bar tools.
  • Water station and recyclable cups (limited glassware is available for an additional fee).

What you provide: You provide the alcohol and a day banquet license, which can easily be obtained on the Washington State liquor board website for around $10.

Consultation & Menu Design: You will have a consultation and tasting with Jesse to go over the bar menu. He will help you calculate exactly how much alcohol you will need based on your guest count, length of reception or party, and preferences.

Recommendation: We highly recommend choosing one or two signature drinks for your event in addition to standard beer and wine. This keeps the service streamlined and creates an exceptionally elegant affair.

Pricing, Travel & Bartending

We charge for the rental of Mobile Bartender, set up, breakdown, and professional bartending service.

  • Travel Radius: Our price includes travel within 25 miles of the North Kitsap Area. We will gladly travel outside this range for an additional fee of $1/mile. Please check the Investment page for specific pricing and add-ons.
  • Bartending Service: $50/hr per bartender. The number of bartenders needed depends on your guest count. For example, 2 bartenders can seamlessly manage up to 150 guests. More guests = more bartenders.

Please contact us for custom pricing details as we love working closely with you on what you need for your specific event!

As much as we would love to, we cannot take Mobile Bartender to the San Juan Islands due to the precariousness of the ferry and the requirement to stay overnight.

However, we can travel to Vashon and Whidbey Island (additional fee applies for the ferry cost).

Lastly, our "travel outside of 25 miles of Kitsap Area" is within reason, meaning within a 60–90 minute drive of North Kitsap. (The Sidecar is a vintage antique vehicle, so we drive very carefully when hauling her!)

Alcohol & State Laws

As it stands, Washington state liquor laws strictly prevent us from selling alcohol directly. This is why we require the Host(s) to provide the booze prior to the event. We handle everything thereafter to make sure you can enjoy your celebration without any hassle.

Non-Profit Exception: We can accommodate a cash bar for a registered non-profit fundraiser where 100% of the proceeds go directly to the non-profit organization.

Payments & Policies

We require a 30% non-refundable deposit along with a signed contract to officially save and secure your date on our calendar.

The remaining payment in full is due the day before your event.

Licensing & Safety

Yes, absolutely! We are fully licensed and insured. Furthermore, all of our professional bartenders carry a Class 12 permit to serve legally and responsibly in the state of Washington.
